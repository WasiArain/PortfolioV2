About Me

I'm a Front-End Web Developer obsessed with one thing: making websites that don't just look good, but perform. My specialty is high-end UI design — advanced CSS animations, pixel-perfect responsiveness, and fully custom Elementor builds that break away from generic templates and give brands an identity that actually stands out.


I hold a BS in Information Technology from Sindh Agriculture University, Tando Jam (2020–2024), which gave me the technical foundation — but the real education came from shipping real projects for real clients.


Since 2022, I've worked as a freelancer for international clients, specializing in E-Commerce development across WordPress (WooCommerce), Shopify, Wix, and Webflow. I don't force every project onto one platform — I choose the right tool for the job and build stores designed to convert, not just exist.


I've also sharpened my craft across teams at ArtStone Digital, Miloady Designs, Technovative Labs, StepTech Solutions, and dotBlue Digital, refining scalable front-end architecture and advanced animation systems across dozens of production sites.


I care more about getting it right than getting it done fast. Every project I take on gets the same standard: pixel-perfect execution, buttery-smooth animations, and a final product my clients are genuinely excited to show off.
